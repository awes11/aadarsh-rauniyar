About

Building systems you can rely on

People often come to me when things feel messy or overwhelming—when spreadsheets multiply, when reports take too long, when the data just doesn't make sense anymore.

My role is to slow things down, ask the right questions, and build systems that bring clarity. I focus on solutions that are simple to understand, easy to maintain, and genuinely useful for the people who use them every day.

I've spent the past two years working with operational and business data—cleaning datasets, building dashboards, automating repetitive tasks, and helping teams make better decisions with the information they have.

I hold a degree in Computing from Leeds Beckett University and am certified through IBM's Data Analyst Professional program.
